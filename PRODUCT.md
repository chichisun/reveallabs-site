# PRODUCT.md — reveal.

## Product Purpose
Reveal is the auditing solution for single and multi-unit restaurant operations. It connects read-only to a restaurant's bank, POS, delivery apps, and the paperwork the owner already keeps (invoices, leases, contracts), then checks every payment in and out against what was agreed. When a number is wrong (a short delivery payout, a vendor price creep, a lease charge over the escalation terms), Reveal flags it with proof and helps recover the money. Pre-launch: the site's only conversion is an email waitlist.

## Users
Non-technical independent restaurant owners (single location or a few). They run on instinct: sales in the POS, vendors in their phones, renewal dates in their heads. They skim on a phone between services, late at night. They distrust software jargon and respect plain talk about money. Proof beats promises: one real number ($1,297.87 recovered at Tuk Tuk Thai Grill in month one) carries more weight than any adjective.

## Brand and Tone
- Calm, premium, plain-spoken. Sweetgreen, not Linear. The register mirrors supy.io: category-defining statements, short functional sentences, zero cleverness.
- Copy is locked and minimal ("simple and concise enough to read and understand"). One short sentence per card body. No em dashes anywhere, ever. "Caught" is Reveal's verb.
- AI is mentioned exactly twice on the homepage (hero sub, how-it-works beat 2). Never add more.
- Motion philosophy: intentional and cohesive, never extravagant. Sequential one-thing-at-a-time beats. Scroll-scrubbed storytelling for how-it-works (approved choreography, do not alter).

## Visual System
- Palette: warm cream neutrals (#F2F1ED base, #FCFBFA lifted) with deep greens (#1E3522 to #355E3B) and a soft green accent (#8BAF8F, #E8F0E9). Amber (#C08532) for warnings, brick red (#C23B22) for errors. Dark green surfaces get a radial light bloom + deep vignette for depth.
- Type: Fustat everywhere (variable 200-800). Big, light headlines with tight tracking; muted grey subs. Numbers in tabular figures on shared right edges; inactive numbers grey.
- Shapes: rounded inset section cards (44px radius) on cream; pills for chips/badges/CTAs; paperwork-styled cards (statements, invoices, receipts) as product vignettes.

## Anti-references
- Dev-tool dark-neon aesthetics (Linear, Vercel-style black).
- Infomercial urgency, fake scarcity, exclamation marks.
- Generic AI-slop patterns: glassmorphism-by-default, gradient text, identical icon-card grids, hero-metric templates, side-stripe alert borders.
- AI-generated faces or people imagery (real photography only; AI imagery only for objects/atmosphere).

## Strategic Principles
- Feel the problem, then visualize the solution. Problem section comes before features.
- Every benefit shows what Reveal DOES about a problem, not just that it detects it.
- Risk reversal closes: "Read-only. Fifteen minutes to connect. If Reveal finds nothing, you've lost nothing."
